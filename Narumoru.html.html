About Our Church

Narumoru Repentance and Holiness Church welcomes people to worship God, learn the Bible, seek repentance and grow in faith.

Our Mission

To preach the Word of God, teach genuine repentance and encourage a life of holiness.

Our Vision

To see lives transformed through Jesus Christ, prayer, discipleship and faithful service.

Our Faith

We believe in Jesus Christ, the authority of Scripture, salvation and godly living.

Our Church Leader
